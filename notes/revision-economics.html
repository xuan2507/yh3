Economics 9708 - Last Minute Revision

Condensed essentials for exam day. For full notes, click "Full Notes" above.

Must-Know Diagrams & Concepts

Key formulas:

PED = (% change Qd) / (% change P)
YED = (% change Qd) / (% change income)
XED = (% change Qd of A) / (% change P of B)
GDP = C + I + G + (X - M)
Consumer surplus = area below demand, above price
Producer surplus = area above supply, below price
Profit max: MC = MR
Revenue max: MR = 0

Microeconomics - Quick Reference

Elasticity:

  • PED > 1 = elastic (price up, revenue down)
  • PED < 1 = inelastic (price up, revenue up)
  • PED = 0 = perfectly inelastic (vertical)
  • PED = infinity = perfectly elastic (horizontal)

Determinants of PED: substitutes, necessity vs luxury, % of income, time period

Market structures comparison:

Types of efficiency:

  • Allocative: P = MC (social welfare maximised)
  • Productive: min AC
  • Dynamic: innovation over time
  • X-inefficiency: costs above minimum (no competition)

Market failure:

  • Externalities (spillover costs/benefits)
  • Public goods (non-excludable, non-rival)
  • Merit goods (underconsumed - info failure)
  • Demerit goods (overconsumed - info failure)
  • Monopoly power

Macroeconomics - Quick Reference

Macro objectives:

  1. Economic growth (real GDP increase)
  2. Low unemployment
  3. Low inflation (~2% target)
  4. Balance of payments equilibrium
  5. Redistribution of income

Inflation causes:

  • Demand-pull: AD > AS (too much money chasing too few goods)
  • Cost-push: rising costs (wages, raw materials)
  • Monetary: excessive money supply
  • Imported: currency depreciation

Unemployment types:

  • Frictional: between jobs (short-term)
  • Structural: skills/location mismatch
  • Cyclical: recession, low AD (cured by demand-side policies)
  • Seasonal: seasonal industries

AD = C + I + G + (X - M)

Shift AD right: increase C, I, G, X or decrease M

Automatic stabilisers: Progressive taxes, unemployment benefits. Work without government action.

Crowding out: Government borrowing raises interest rates, reducing private investment.

International Trade

Comparative advantage: Produce where opportunity cost is lowest. Specialise and trade.

Protectionism methods: Tariffs, quotas, embargoes, subsidies, administrative barriers

Arguments for protection: Infant industries, domestic jobs, strategic industries, dumping prevention

Arguments against: Reduced efficiency, higher prices, retaliation, less choice

Exchange rates:

  • Appreciation: currency stronger. Cheaper imports, dearer exports. Reduces inflation.
  • Depreciation: currency weaker. Dearer imports, cheaper exports. May cause inflation.
  • J-curve: After depreciation, current account worsens before improving (contracts in short run)

Balance of payments:

  • Current account = trade in goods + services + income + transfers
  • Deficit remedies: expenditure-reducing (contractionary policy) or expenditure-switching (devaluation, protectionism)

Essay Writing (25 marks)

Structure: Introduction (3-4 marks) -> Analysis paragraphs (12-14 marks) -> Evaluation (6-8 marks) -> Conclusion (2-3 marks)

Introduction: Define key terms, set context, state approach

Analysis paragraph structure (PEEL+):

  • Point - clear topic sentence
  • Explanation - economic theory
  • Example - real world
  • Link - back to question
  • + Diagram - labelled, referenced

Evaluation phrases:

  • "However, this depends on..."
  • "In the short run... but in the long run..."
  • "The magnitude/significance of this effect..."
  • "From the perspective of [stakeholder]..."
  • "This assumes ceteris paribus, but in reality..."
  • "A limitation of this policy is..."

Conclusion: Make a judgment. Prioritise factors. Consider context (developed vs developing country).

Data Response Tips

  1. Read the question first - know what to look for
  2. Use the data: Quote figures, calculate % changes
  3. % change = [(new-old)/old] x 100
  4. Real value = (Nominal/Price Index) x 100
  5. Apply theory: Use relevant economic concepts
  6. Evaluate: Consider limitations of data, alternative explanations

Command words:

  • "Explain" = reasons why
  • "Analyse" = detailed with theory + diagrams
  • "Discuss" = both sides
  • "Evaluate" = make judgments
  • "Assess" = judge importance
  • "To what extent" = balance, then conclude

Quick Tips: Always define key terms. Use diagrams with labels (shift arrows!). Include real-world examples (countries, events). Evaluate every major point. In 25-mark essays, aim for 3-4 evaluation points. Watch your time - 25-mark essay = ~35 minutes.
