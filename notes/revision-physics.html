Must-Know Formulas

v = u + at
s = ut + 1/2 at^2
v^2 = u^2 + 2as
F = ma
W = mg
p = mv
KE = 1/2 mv^2
GPE = mgh
P = W/t = Fv
efficiency = (useful out / total in) x 100%
v = f x lambda
I = V/R (Ohm's Law)
P = VI = I^2R = V^2/R
R = rho L / A
E = mc^2

Kinematics (SUVAT)

When to use each formula:

  • v = u + at - No displacement
  • s = (u+v)t/2 - No acceleration
  • s = ut + 1/2 at^2 - No final velocity
  • v^2 = u^2 + 2as - No time

Projectile: Resolve into horizontal (constant v) and vertical (a = g = 9.81 m/s^2)

At max height: v_vertical = 0

Forces, Momentum & Dynamics

Newton's 2nd: F = ma

Newton's 3rd: Action-reaction pairs act on DIFFERENT bodies

Momentum: p = mv. Conserved in collisions if no external force.

Elastic collision: KE conserved. Relative speed of approach = separation.

Impulse: Ft = change in momentum. Area under F-t graph.

Moment: F x d (perpendicular distance). Sum clockwise = sum anticlockwise.

Work, Energy & Power

Work: W = Fs cos(theta)

Conservation of energy: Total energy constant. Lost GPE = gained KE (if no friction).

Power: Rate of doing work. P = W/t = Fv

Efficiency: Always < 100%. Heat/sound losses.

Waves & Optics

Wave equation: v = f x lambda

EM spectrum order: Radio -> Microwave -> IR -> Visible -> UV -> X-ray -> Gamma

All EM: c = 3 x 10^8 m/s in vacuum, transverse, no medium needed

Double slit: w = lambda D / a (fringe spacing)

Stationary waves: Node spacing = lambda/2. String fixed both ends: L = n(lambda/2)

Diffraction: Most when gap ~ wavelength

Electricity

Ohm's Law: V = IR

Series: R_total = R1 + R2 + R3

Parallel: 1/R = 1/R1 + 1/R2

Potential divider: V_out = V_in x R2/(R1+R2)

EMF: E = V + Ir (terminal p.d. + lost volts)

Kirchhoff 1: Sum currents in = sum currents out

Kirchhoff 2: Sum EMFs = sum p.d.s around loop

I-V characteristics: Ohmic = straight line, Filament = curve (R increases), Thermistor = R decreases with temp, Diode = one way only

Particle Physics

Proton: uud, charge +1

Neutron: udd, charge 0

Quark charges: Up = +2/3, Down = -1/3, Strange = -1/3

Alpha decay: A-4, Z-2

Beta- decay: A same, Z+1 (neutron -> proton + electron + antineutrino)

Activity: A = lambda N. lambda = ln(2)/t_1/2

Half-life: Time for activity/count to halve. Constant for a given isotope.

1 u = 931.5 MeV/c^2

Quick Tips: Always check units. Draw diagrams. Show working. Use significant figures appropriately. In mechanics, draw force diagrams. In electricity, redraw circuit diagrams clearly.
